Delete your account

Mental Penalty

In the app (instant)

  1. Open the Mental Penalty app and sign in.
  2. Tap "Delete account" at the bottom of the main menu.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

If you can't access the app

Email mentalpenalti@gmail.com from the email address on your account, include your username and ask us to delete your account. We'll complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your username, email address, password, win/loss statistics, coins, purchased and equipped items and all sessions are permanently deleted and you're removed from the leaderboard. No copy is kept. Deleted accounts can't be restored and unspent coins are not refunded.
