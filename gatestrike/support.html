FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. The game requires no account and can be played offline.

Where is my progress stored?
Coins, upgrades and levels are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

I bought coins but did not receive them.
Close and reopen the game. If the problem continues, email us with your purchase receipt.

Are ads mandatory?
No. Ads are shown only when you tap "Watch Ad" to get a reward.
