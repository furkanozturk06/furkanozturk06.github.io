Frequently asked

My "Remove ads" purchase is gone.

On Android it comes back by itself when the game opens. On iPhone, tap Restore purchases in Settings. You must be signed in with the same Google or Apple account.

I bought a coin pack but did not get the coins.

Close and reopen the game; unfinished purchases are delivered at launch. If they still do not arrive, write to us with the order number from your receipt.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone), your progress is backed up and comes to a new device signed in with the same account.
